## Coordination and good issues

Coordinating work has been relatively challenging. While we have been able to mitigate this through the use of [contribution guidelines](/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [issue templates](/.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE), these have not always been followed consistently by all group members. This occasionally resulted in inconsistencies in how contributions were structured and documented, creating additional work when reviewing and integrating changes. We learned that establishing guidelines alone is insufficient; they must also be consistently adopted and maintained by the entire group.
